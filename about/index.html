About

I am a Technical Strategy Director at Everpure , focused on new product introduction and cloud. I sit between product engineering and the field, which means I spend my time turning what customers actually need into reference architectures, demos and labs, and turning what the field runs into input for the roadmap.

I have spent my whole career in Central Florida infrastructure. It started on the Orlando Health service desk in 2008 and went through systems engineering, VMware technical marketing and field solutions architecture to get here.

This blog is where the hands-on work ends up: storage, cloud, virtualization and whatever I happened to be breaking that week.
